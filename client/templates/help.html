Quick Start — Check Out Items

1

Scan the Library Card

Scan or type the patron's library card number in the Library Card field, then press Enter or click Look Up Patron.

2

Scan the Item Barcode

Scan the item barcode in the action panel, then choose the loan period (1, 2, or 3 weeks) from the Loan period selector. The default is 2 weeks.

3

Submit

Press Enter or click Checkout. The item appears in the patron's active list instantly and the barcode field clears for the next scan.

Loan Period

Use the Loan period selector when checking out or renewing an item. The selected period applies to the next action — change it before submitting.

Option Due date
1 week Today + 7 days
2 weeks default Today + 14 days
3 weeks Today + 21 days

Actions

↓

Checkout

Loans an item to the patron. The due date is calculated from the Loan period selector (default 2 weeks from today).

↑

Return

Marks an item as returned. Releases it from the patron's account so it can be checked out again. Logs a separate audit entry.

↻

Renew

Extends the due date from today. Set the Loan period before scanning to choose how many weeks the renewal adds.

Item Categories

Select a category when checking out. Used for filtering in History and printed on receipts.

📖 Book (default)
📀 DVD
🎧 Audiobook
📰 Magazine
💾 eBook
📦 Other

Tips & Shortcuts

  • ⌨ Press Enter in any field to submit — no mouse required for the full workflow.
  • 🔄 After each checkout the barcode field auto-clears so you can scan the next item immediately.
  • 🖨 Print Receipt opens a PDF listing all currently-out items with due dates.
  • 🔍 History lets you view all transactions per patron and filter by action type.
  • ⚠ Late items display in red. The patron card's Late count also turns red when overdue items exist.

Registering a New Patron

Open the Register page from the navigation bar, or click the link that appears automatically when the checkout screen detects an unknown card. The library card number is assigned automatically — no need to enter one. Fill in the patron's name, date of birth, and optionally their email and phone, then click Register Patron.

Required fields:

  • * Last name
  • * First name
  • * Date of birth

Names are stored uppercase in LAST, FIRST format.

Auto-generated card numbers

  • • Always 14 digits, assigned sequentially
  • • Starts at 10000000000001 if the database is empty
  • • Displayed read-only on the form so you can note it for the patron
  • • Each number is unique — no duplicates possible

Adding Items to the Catalog

Open the Register page from the navigation bar and choose the Book tab. Scan or type the item's ISBN. The system will automatically look up the title, author, and category from Google Books. Review the auto-filled details, correct anything if needed, then click Add to Catalog.

Accepted barcode formats:

  • • ISBN-10 — 10 digits (older books)
  • • ISBN-13 — 13 digits (modern books, DVDs)
  • • Library barcode — 14-digit EAN sticker

Note: items added here can then be checked out on the main screen by scanning the same barcode.

ISBN auto-fill

  • • Triggered automatically when you enter a 10- or 13-digit number
  • • Populates title, author, and category from Google Books
  • • If no match is found, fill in the details manually
  • • All auto-filled fields are editable before saving
  • • After saving, click Add another item to continue without navigating away

Statistics Dashboard

Open the Stats page from the navigation bar for a real-time overview of the library.

Metrics shown:

  • • Total patrons registered
  • • Unique items in the catalog
  • • Items currently checked out
  • • Overdue items (click tile to expand full list)
  • • Checkout activity — today, last 7 days, all time
  • • Active items broken down by category
  • • Catalog occupancy rate
  • • Top 5 most-borrowed items

Overdue detail view

Click the Overdue tile (highlighted in red when items are overdue) to expand a table showing every overdue item with the patron's name, card number, book title, barcode, due date, and days overdue. Use the Refresh button at the top to reload all statistics.

Patron Cards & Email Receipts

After registering a patron, the success panel offers two printouts and one email option:

  • * Print Patron Card — opens a PDF with the patron's name, library card number, and a scannable Code-128 barcode
  • * Email to Patron — sends the same PDF to the email address on file (requires SMTP to be configured)
  • * Continue to Checkout — drops the librarian back on the checkout screen with the new card pre-loaded

On the checkout screen, when a patron has items out, an Email Receipt button sits next to Print Receipt. It sends the same PDF as an attachment to the patron's email. If there's no email on file for the patron, the button is disabled.

Email features are disabled when SMTP_HOST is empty in your environment. See the README's Email configuration section for setup.

Editing Patron Information

Open the History page, look up the patron by card or name, and click the Edit button next to the stats panel. You can change the patron's name, date of birth, email, and phone — leave a field blank to clear it, or leave it alone to preserve the existing value. First and last name cannot be blanked out.

Editing Catalog Items

Open the Catalog page, search for the item, and click its row to expand it. Click the Edit button in the top-right of the expanded panel to open an inline edit form. You can update the item's barcode, title, author, and category. Click Save Changes to write the update to the database, or Cancel to discard it.

Notes

  • • The new barcode must be a valid ISBN-10, ISBN-13, or 14-digit library barcode and must not already be in use
  • • Loan history is preserved when a barcode is corrected — records link by internal ID, not barcode
  • • Title and author can be left blank to clear the field
  • • Changes take effect immediately — the expanded panel refreshes automatically

Searching & Autofill

Both the checkout screen's item field and the Catalog search box show a live autofill dropdown as you type. Each suggestion lists the barcode, title, and author, along with current availability (e.g. 3/5 available).

Keyboard navigation

  • ↑ / ↓ — highlight the previous / next suggestion
  • Enter — select the highlighted suggestion (or submit if none)
  • Esc — close the dropdown

Wildcard barcode search

Append * to a barcode prefix in the Catalog search to match every book whose barcode starts with that value — e.g. 456000034* returns every item from that supplier batch. Plain queries (without *) also match against title and author.

Inventory & Multiple Copies

Every book tracks total copies, available copies, and checked-out count. A book with 5 copies can be checked out to up to 5 patrons at the same time — or a single patron can hold multiple copies (e.g. a teacher borrowing a class set).

When adding a book

  • * Enter a Quantity in the Add form (default 1)
  • * The new book starts with all copies available

Adjusting inventory later

  • * Look up the book in Manage Existing Book
  • * Use Add more copies when a new shipment arrives
  • * Use Set total copies to correct counts after a loss or withdrawal
  • * Cannot reduce below the number currently checked out

Seeing who has a book

The Manage panel lists every patron currently holding a copy, grouped by patron with a copies count badge. Each row has a Return 1 button to return one specific copy back to inventory. The inventory tiles update live.

Manual Returns (Override)

If a book was returned but not logged at the time, a librarian can mark it as returned from multiple places in the system. Each method calls the same return API and creates an audit trail.

Checkout screen

Look up the patron, then click the Return button next to any item in the Currently Checked Out table.

History page

Look up the patron by card or name. Active items appear above the transaction log, each with a Return button.

Books page

Look up the book by barcode in the Manage Existing Book section. If it is checked out, a Return Item button appears.

Managing Books

The Catalog page lets you search for and manage existing items. New items are added from the Register page under the Book tab.

From the expanded row you can:

  • * Edit — update the item's title, author, and category in place
  • * Return — mark a checked-out copy as returned
  • * Add / set copies — adjust the physical inventory count
  • * Archive — soft-delete an item (must not be checked out)
  • * Reactivate — restore an archived item to active status

The expanded panel shows the item's current status (active/archived, checked out or not) and only displays relevant action buttons.

Archiving Patrons

When a patron no longer uses the library, their account can be archived instead of deleted. This preserves all loan history and transaction records.

Rules:

  • * All items must be returned before archiving
  • * Archived patrons cannot check out items
  • * Patron data and history are fully preserved
  • * Accounts can be reactivated at any time

API: POST /api/patrons/<card>/archive and POST /api/patrons/<card>/reactivate

Archiving Books

Books that are lost, damaged, or withdrawn from circulation can be archived. The catalog record and all loan history are preserved.

Rules:

  • * The item must be returned (not checked out) before archiving
  • * Archived items cannot be checked out
  • * Catalog data and loan history are fully preserved
  • * Items can be reactivated at any time

API: POST /api/books/<barcode>/archive and POST /api/books/<barcode>/reactivate

Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to expand the answer.

Can the same item be checked out twice at once?
No. The system prevents duplicate active checkouts for the same barcode. The item must be returned before it can be checked out again.
What happens if I try to return an item that isn't checked out?
The system shows an error message. Only items with an active checkout record can be returned.
How do I choose a different loan period?
Use the Loan period dropdown that appears under the barcode field when the Checkout or Renew tab is active. Select 1, 2, or 3 weeks before submitting. The selection persists between scans so you only need to change it when the period changes.
Where does the receipt go?
Clicking Print Receipt generates a PDF in a new tab containing all of the patron's currently-checked-out items with their due dates. Print or save it from the browser.
Can a patron have multiple items out at once?
Yes. There is no system-enforced limit. Scan each item barcode one at a time to add them all to the same patron's account.
How do I customise the background or icon?
Drop image files into the client/static/images/ folder:
  • background.jpg (or .png / .webp) — full-page background, shown semi-transparently.
  • icon.png (or .svg / .ico) — replaces the default book icon in the header and browser tab.
Restart the server after adding images — no code changes needed.
Is patron email stored anywhere external?
No. Email is optional and stored only in the local SQLite database (data/library.db). It is never transmitted to any external service.
Do I need to add an item to the catalog before checking it out?
No — items are created automatically the first time their barcode is scanned on the checkout screen. The Catalog page is useful when you want to pre-populate the catalog with titles, authors, and categories before any checkouts occur, or to enrich existing records with ISBN metadata.
What happens if the ISBN auto-fill doesn't find a result?
The form shows a "No metadata found" notice and leaves the title, author, and category fields blank for you to fill in manually. The item can still be added — metadata is optional. This can happen for very new publications, regional editions, or items without an ISBN in Google Books.
How do I see which items are overdue?
Open the Stats page and click the Overdue tile. A table expands showing every overdue item with the patron's name, full card number, book title, barcode, due date, and how many days past-due it is. Individual overdue items are also highlighted in red on the main checkout screen when a patron is looked up.
What happens when I archive a patron or book?
Archiving is a soft-delete — the record is marked inactive but all data and loan history are preserved. Archived patrons cannot check out items and archived books cannot be lent. Either can be reactivated at any time to restore full functionality.
Can I archive a patron who still has books checked out?
No. All items must be returned before a patron account can be archived. Similarly, a book that is currently checked out cannot be archived until it is returned.
Can I change a patron's library card number after registration?
Not through the UI currently. Card numbers are assigned once at registration. If a replacement card is needed, contact your system administrator to update the record directly in the database (data/library.db).
